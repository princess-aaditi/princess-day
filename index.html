    Royal Proclamation

    On this Princess Day, Aaditi is crowned

    By her command, Shree shall deliver:

      Aaditi,

      I don’t say this often enough, so I’m saying it plainly. You are the first person I want to tell everything to, and the one I want beside me when I have nothing to say at all.

      Knowing you’re unwell makes me restless, because somewhere along the way your happiness became mine. So rest. Sleep as long as you need. Let me carry the worry.

      Get well soon, my princess. I’m waiting for your laugh, your sparkle and your stubbornness, all of it. Princess Day lasts one day. You are my princess for all the rest of them.

      I love you.
